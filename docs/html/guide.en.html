1. About the game

A-Zhe is 27. After five years at a logistics company, the company suddenly shut down, and the severance pay covered only two months' rent. This morning A-Zhe stands at an MRT station exit with a backpack, NT$1,200 in cash, and a phone cut off for unpaid bills.

Your job is to guide A-Zhe:

  • survive day by day, finding food and a place to sleep;
  • get back on track with help from social welfare;
  • learn a skill, find work and rent a home;
  • read the economy and the news, and start a business at the right moment;
  • and finally become a tycoon worth NT$50 million — or live a completely different life.

The game has a fully walkable 3D city, 10 countries, 16 endings, LAN multiplayer and 5 languages.

2. Install and launch

Requirements - Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit). - A graphics card that supports Vulkan (any recent NVIDIA, AMD or Intel GPU). - About 200 MB of disk space.

Install 1. Download StreetTycoon_Setup_1.1.0.exe from Releases. 2. Double-click it. No administrator rights are needed. 3. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click More info → Run anyway. The installer is not code-signed, which is why the warning appears. 4. Launch 街角翻身記 from the desktop or the Start menu.

First launch: the game starts in Traditional Chinese. Click 語言 Language on the title screen and choose English. Your choice is remembered.

Uninstall: Windows Settings → Apps → 街角翻身記 Street Tycoon. Your saves are kept.

3. Starting a new life

The title screen has Continue, Start a New Life, Load Save, Language and Multiplayer.

A new game asks you to choose:

  1. Country: Taiwan, Japan, South Korea, China, USA, Canada, UK, Germany, France or Australia. Prices, wages, currency, cities, transport and holidays all differ, and characters and places are localized.
  2. Difficulty
Difficulty What changes
Simple Double starting cash; extra weekly welfare when your savings are low
Normal The original design
Hard Rent rises an extra 30% every month; accidents and illness are more frequent
Hell Start broke with credit card debt; rent rises an extra 50%
  1. Your character: look, full name, nickname and starting age (18–45).

5. The screen

  • Top bar:
  • day, date and city; the four time slots (Morning, Noon, Evening, Late Night) and a clock;
  • Cash, Savings and Net Worth;
  • the Multiplayer, Phone, Life, Map and Menu buttons.
  • Left panel:
  • Fullness, Stamina, Mood, Health and Hygiene;
  • five skills: Labor, Service, Computer, Cooking and Business;
  • age, home, job, credit score, network and more.
  • Right panel: minimap, current conditions, news forecast, city development, and your diary (everything that happened).
  • Bottom panel: what you can do at your current location. Each card shows the cost, how many time slots it takes, and why you can't do it yet. Hover over a card for the full description, and press Collapse at the bottom right to shrink the panel and see more of the city.
  • Places: besides the Social Welfare Center, Employment Service, Bank and others, there are a Hospital (doctor, health check, counselling), Police Station (report scams, turn yourself in, criminal record certificate), District Court (watch trials, debt mediation), Gym, Pet Shop and Brokerage.
  • 3D city:
  • seasons change the city: snow in winter in places like Japan and Korea, cherry blossoms in March and April, and autumn leaves;
  • festivals differ by country, and the right panel shows the current one:
    • Taiwan and China: red lanterns for Lunar New Year, golden lanterns for the Mid-Autumn Festival;
    • Japan: red-and-white chochin lanterns at New Year and summer festivals (with fireworks), blue-and-white Christmas illuminations;
    • Korea: colorful lanterns for Seollal, Chuseok and the Lotus Lantern Festival;
    • USA and Canada: fireworks on Independence Day and Canada Day, Halloween;
    • UK: Bonfire Night fireworks; Germany: Oktoberfest and Christmas markets; France: Bastille Day fireworks; Australia: Australia Day fireworks and a summer Christmas;
    • every country has fireworks on New Year's Eve;
  • you are the one with the amber arrow overhead;
  • round icons above buildings show what a place is for ($ = earn money, Zz = sleep, Eat, Aid, Edu, …);
  • once you open businesses, your shops appear in the city with your company sign and a ★ marker.

6. How a day works

  • A day has four slots: Morning, Noon, Evening, Late Night. Working, classes and odd jobs use up slots; eating and shopping usually don't.
  • Some things only happen at certain times, for example night-shift security late at night, or wholesale goods early in the morning.
  • When the slots run out, or you press End the day and find a place to sleep, choose where to spend the night:
  • a park bench (free, but risky and bad for your health);
  • a shelter, an internet café or a hostel;
  • your rented room, or a home you own.
  • Each morning a receipt shows yesterday's income, expenses and events.
  • Mornings sometimes bring events: illness, a lost wallet, a friend, a scam call, a rival's move. You must pick an option to close one, and your choices shape what happens later.

8. The six chapters

Chapter Goal
1. Street Survival Survive the first five days and see a social worker at the Social Welfare Center
2. Back on My Feet Find your first job and rent a room
3. Skilled Trade Earn a vocational certificate or a university degree
4. First Pot of Gold Open your first business and reach a net worth of NT$100,000
5. Riding the Wave Reach a net worth of NT$3,000,000
6. Business Tycoon Reach a net worth of NT$50,000,000

Amounts are in NT$ and converted to the local currency in other countries.

A typical path to the top 1. Social Welfare Center: open a case with a social worker to unlock shelter beds, emergency aid, work-relief jobs, the food bank and study allowances. 2. Odd jobs: day labor, moving goods and recycling at the Employment Service. With a phone, the Gig App lets you do deliveries. 3. Rent a home: you need an address to apply for full-time jobs, open a business or take out a loan. 4. Learn a skill: take courses and earn a certificate at the Job Training Center, or go to university (student loans available). 5. Get a better job: regular jobs, or after a degree, a corporate job with a promotion ladder. 6. Start a business at Start a Business: begin with an Online Shop or a Night Market Stall, then grow into a Bento Shop, Bubble Tea Shop, Delivery Fleet, Trading Company and Supermarket Chain. 7. Expand: open branches, fight business wars, manage staff and go public.

9. Jobs, university and licenses

  • Regular jobs: apply at the Employment Service. Skip work three days in a row and you're fired.
  • University: medicine, law, architecture, computer science, accounting, education, nursing and more.
  • You pay your own tuition; student loans are available.
  • Evening classes let working students attend.
  • There are two exams a year. Answer the questions yourself, or let the game answer based on how well you prepared.
  • Fail a subject and you retake it. Fail more than half your subjects two semesters in a row and you're expelled. Run out of time and you graduate late.
  • Doctors, lawyers, architects, accountants, teachers and nurses must pass a licensing exam after graduating.
  • You can't work in a profession without the matching degree.
  • Corporate jobs: with a degree, apply at Corporate HQ and climb the salary ladder. Big companies won't hire anyone with a criminal record.
  • Vocational licenses (Job Training Center): chef, forklift, security guard and tour guide. Each unlocks well-paid work: catering chef, forklift driver, night-shift security, leading tour groups.

10. Business and business wars

  • Open / Expand at Start a Business. Profits and losses are settled automatically every night. Inspect Stores boosts that day's revenue by 15%.
  • Conditions and news:
  • Typhoons, cold snaps, heat waves, epidemics, tourism booms, inflation, recessions and housing booms all affect different industries.
  • The news forecast warns you in advance. Prepare early to profit: stock raincoats before a typhoon, gift boxes before the holidays.
  • Business War Center:
  • Every industry has AI rivals, and market share decides your revenue.
  • Advertise, propose alliances or acquire rivals. Allies don't start price wars, but an ally can betray you.
  • Dirty Tactics (illegal or unethical): online smear campaigns, corporate spies, poaching managers, predatory pricing, reporting rivals, bribing suppliers, vandalism.
  • Get caught and you'll be sued, fined, lose reputation, and possibly get a criminal record.
  • The deeper the grudge, the harder rivals hit back.
  • Staff Management:
  • Set low, market-rate or high pay for each industry. Morale changes revenue.
  • Very low morale leads to strikes, and low pay invites labor inspections and fines.
  • Training improves service, and a professional manager stops employee theft.
  • AI tycoon rivals: three AI characters also start on the street, with steady, risk-taking or ruthless personalities. They open shops in your industries, offer joint ventures, and can go broke or get arrested. Check the Rich List on your phone.
  • Gangs: once you own three or more shops, local gangsters come for protection money. Pay, call the police, hire a rival gang, or hire security.

11. Money: bank, stocks, insurance

  • Bank:
  • Savings can't be stolen at night.
  • Youth startup loans and secured loans are available; interest is charged every 30 days.
  • Unpaid utility bills, health insurance and taxes become debts and lower your credit score.
  • Stocks & Investing (open a brokerage account at the bank; you need an address):
  • Trade 8 stocks, an index fund, and the very volatile StreetCoin crypto.
  • News moves prices: epidemics lift biotech stocks, typhoons hit shipping and tourism.
  • A large enough company can go public to raise money. If your stake drops below 50%, you risk a hostile takeover.
  • Landlord (Real Estate Agency):
  • Buy studios, flats or townhouses to rent out; rent comes in on the 5th of each month. The higher the rent, the harder it is to find tenants and the more likely they pay late.
  • Expect late rent, leaks and damage. Foreclosure auctions occasionally offer 35% off, but the former owner may still be living there.
  • When prices get very high, the housing bubble can burst.
  • Overseas expansion (Start a Business): with 10 shops at home and Business skill 55, open branches in other countries. Profits are converted at the exchange rate, and you'll face currency drops, trade-war tariffs and local regulations.
  • Luxury goods (Clothing Store):
  • A Swiss watch, fine art, a supercar, a yacht or a hilltop mansion raises your fame, mood, network and followers.
  • They cost upkeep, and the more famous you are, the more likely you get kidnapped for ransom. A bodyguard prevents kidnapping.
  • Art prices go up and down.
  • Annual income tax:
  • Every May you file last year's income, with progressive tax brackets.
  • File honestly yourself, hire an accountant to cut your tax legally, or under-report and risk a tax audit later.
  • Insurance:
  • Business fire and disaster cover pays 90% of damage to your shops.
  • Home earthquake cover pays in full.
  • Medical cover means you pay only 30% of medical bills.
  • Accident cover pays out after a traffic accident.

12. Everyday life

  • Food: from convenience-store bento boxes to fine dining.
  • Group dinners: split the bill, treat your friends, or let them treat you.
  • Pets: adopt a stray cat or dog on the street. Pets lift your mood and protect against depression, but they need food every day and a vet when sick. Landlords may object, and a dog can scare off burglars.
  • Fitness and looks: jog, go to the gym, get a haircut. Looks and charm improve job interviews, alliance offers, election support and follower growth.
  • Hobbies:
  • Guitar: once you're good enough, go busking at the station.
  • Photography: once you're good enough, shoot weddings.
  • Cooking: good cooks can enter a cooking contest.
  • Social media:
  • Post a short video every day; now and then one goes viral. Livestream late at night at an internet café for tips.
  • With enough followers, sponsors come calling — but some of their products are dodgy.
  • A shady past can get you cancelled.
  • Love and family: meet a partner, marry, have children, and care for an ill parent.
  • Raising children (Life panel → Children, or "Spend time with your kids" at the Street Park):
  • Each child has a name, a talent (business, study, art or sport), grades and a bond with you.
  • Children grow up four times faster than real time, through baby, kindergarten, elementary, high school and university to adulthood.
  • Time together builds your bond, cram school raises grades, and lessons develop talent. Children with a weak bond may rebel as teenagers.
  • As adults they choose a path: take over the family business, chase their own dream, or strike out with seed money from you.
  • Mini-games:
  • Scooter delivery (Street Park, needs a scooter): dodge traffic across three lanes; the longer you last, the more you earn.
  • Cooking contest (Food Court, once every 7 days): press Space when the marker hits the middle of the heat zone.
  • Underground dice (Street Park, from evening): bet big or small — illegal, and the police may raid it.
  • Day trading (Brokerage or the Stocks panel): buy low and sell high within 30 seconds, at 3× leverage.
  • Scooter: buy a used scooter at the Wholesale Market and take the license test at the Job Training Center. Then you can do scooter deliveries and get to gigs faster.

13. Life's risks

  • Accidents and illness: flu, traffic accidents, food poisoning, toothache, quarantine. Health at zero means hospital.
  • Scams:
  • fake parcel texts, fake prosecutors, AI deepfake calls from "friends", fake investment groups, romance scams, job scams, fake landlords, ATM "cancel the installment plan" scams, bank fraud calls.
  • When in doubt, choose Call 165 or hang up and call the official number yourself. That is usually the safe option.
  • The higher your scam awareness, the more red flags you'll notice in the event text.
  • Disasters:
  • Typhoons can flood the city, and the 3D streets really flood.
  • Earthquakes shake the screen; blackouts turn off every street light.
  • Insurance cuts your losses a lot.
  • City development:
  • A new MRT line raises property prices and foot traffic; a science park boosts sales but pushes up rent.
  • Urban renewal can force you to move out, or bring a big buyout offer if you own a home.
  • A new mall or a declining old district hurts small shops.

14. The grey zone: crime and its consequences

The game lets you go astray, but the consequences are real.

  • Theft and robbery: shoplifting, pickpocketing, burglary, armed robbery.
  • The action card shows your chance of getting caught in red.
  • Police attention and prior convictions make arrest more likely.
  • Underground economy: selling counterfeits, underground money transfers, running an illegal casino behind your shop.
  • Traffic violations: running red lights and speeding on your scooter means more deliveries, but also fines and penalty points. 12 points suspends your license; riding without a license gets your scooter towed; drunk riding gives you a criminal record.
  • Corruption: bribing officials for public contracts, paying off inspectors, lobbying for law changes. Each bribe raises the risk of an anti-corruption raid.
  • Court: when you're arrested or sued, you go to court and choose:
  • plea bargain: a lighter sentence, and first offenders may get probation;
  • public defender: free, but a low chance of winning;
  • regular lawyer: moderate cost, moderate chance;
  • star legal team: expensive, the best chance, but costs morality;
  • settlement: pay damages and avoid a record — not possible for serious crimes.
  • Criminal record: four convictions, or a serious crime after serving time, means the Behind Bars ending. A record also blocks corporate jobs and running for office.

15. Elections and politics

  • Register to run for city council at the Campaign HQ in the Social Welfare Center. You need NT$5 million, non-negative morality, network 40, and no criminal record.
  • The campaign lasts 30 days:
  • street canvassing, campaign rallies, campaign ads;
  • smearing your opponent (you may be sued for defamation);
  • vote buying (a serious crime: get caught and the election is voided and you go to prison).
  • A TV debate and an offer of political donations will come your way.
  • Win more than 50% of the vote on election day. The count has a margin of error, and your opponent campaigns every day too.
  • As a councillor you draw a monthly salary and can propose bills. Then run for mayor: winning gives the Voice of the People ending.

16. Age and endings

  • You age one year every year. After 45, stamina recovers more slowly and health slowly declines; regular exercise slows this down.
  • At 60 you retire and the game ends.
  • After reaching NT$50 million, the second half of life opens, and you choose your final chapter: take the company public, start a foundation, run for office, or hand over to a successor.
  • 16 endings:
Type Endings
Good Business Empire, Philanthropist Tycoon, Renowned Doctor, Top Lawyer, CEO, Educator, Ring the Bell, Voice of the People, Legacy, Comfortable Retirement, Ordinary Retirement
Bad Shady Tycoon, Behind Bars, Back on the Streets, Collapsed from Overwork, A Bleak Old Age
  • Endings you've seen are collected in the Life panel and kept across playthroughs.
  • There are also 27 achievements under Menu → Achievements.

17. Multiplayer

  • Friends on the same local network (same router or Wi-Fi) can play together.
  • One player clicks Multiplayer → Create Room (Host), and the screen shows the host's IP. The others enter that IP and click Join. Everyone lives their own life, and you see each other in the same city.
  • Things players can do to each other:
  • lend money, demand repayment and sue — or refuse to pay;
  • attack another player's company: smear campaigns, poaching, frivolous lawsuits, vandalism (pay damages if you're caught);
  • propose a joint venture that pays dividends daily for 120 days.
  • The Players & Loans panel has a leaderboard ranked by net worth.
  • Run out of cash and savings with debts larger than your assets and you go bankrupt; your debts become bad debts.

18. Settings: language, music, saves

  • Language: 繁體中文, 简体中文, English, 日本語, 한국어. Change it from Language on the title screen or in the Menu.
  • Volume (Menu → Volume):
  • set master, music, ambience and sound effects separately, or mute.
  • 11 background tracks of about 10 minutes each, with three play modes: Auto (by situation), Fixed track and Shuffle.
  • Saves:
  • the game autosaves all the time.
  • three extra save slots are under Menu → Save / Load — handy before a risky move.
  • Phone (press P): Gig App, stocks, bank, social media, news, financial report, Rich List, taxi, map, life, achievements and Scam Guard, all in one place.
  • Settings (Menu → Settings):
  • windowed or fullscreen, window size (1280×720 / 1600×900 / 1920×1080) and UI size.
  • graphics quality: low, medium or high. If the game runs slowly, choose low: it turns off shadows and effects and shows fewer pedestrians.
  • Tutorial: a new game shows tutorial tips at the top of the screen that walk you through your first day. The destination button glows and a light beam marks the spot on the map. Skip it any time, or restart or turn it off in Settings.

19. Tips for new players

  1. On day one, go to the Social Welfare Center and open a case. Shelter beds, the food bank and emergency aid all depend on it.
  2. Put money in the bank: cash gets stolen easily when you sleep in the park.
  3. Save from odd jobs and rent a home quickly: you need an address for full-time jobs, businesses and loans.
  4. Buy a used phone at the Wholesale Market to use the Gig App for deliveries.
  5. Choose a certificate or a degree: certificates are fast; degrees open corporate and professional careers. Working students can take evening classes.
  6. Watch the news forecast: raincoats before typhoons, masks before epidemics, gift boxes before the holidays.
  7. Start your first business small: an Online Shop or Night Market Stall is low-risk. The higher your Business skill, the better your revenue.
  8. Don't underpay staff: the savings rarely cover strikes and labor fines.
  9. Get insurance: once you have several shops, one flood or earthquake can wipe out months of profit.
  10. Always verify suspected scams: if someone wants a transfer, gift cards or a verification code, hang up first.
  11. Watch your age: the game ends at 60, so a younger start gives you more time.
